# 6–9 Opportunity Schema

Opportunities are real-world environments where development can be practiced, not prescriptions that every child should participate in.

```json
{
  "id": "op-6-9-0001",
  "name": "Example opportunity",
  "min_age_months": 72,
  "max_age_months": 119,
  "cost_level": "low|moderate|high|variable",
  "commitment": "one-off|occasional|weekly|seasonal|ongoing",
  "domains": ["character", "social"],
  "skills_practiced": ["teamwork", "responsibility"],
  "setting": "home|school|community|outdoors|organized_activity|other",
  "notes": "Why this opportunity can be useful and what variation to consider."
}
```

Do not imply that a named organization is universally appropriate or available. Local availability, cost, accessibility, culture, family preference, and child interest vary.
