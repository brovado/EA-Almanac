# 6–9 Retrieval Rules

## Age fit
Prefer the narrowest matching window. Broad entries remain eligible when no narrow match exists.

## Category fit
Respect the requested category. Do not use a `teach` entry as an `experiencing` observation merely because the subject overlaps.

## Domain balance
Across a multi-entry snapshot, prefer broad coverage rather than allowing the most heavily researched domain to dominate. Target corpus composition is approximately: academic 15%, character 15%, social 15%, emotional 15%, physical 10%, responsibility 10%, creativity 10%, practical_life 10%.

## Recency
Avoid recently served entries and near-duplicates. Track entry IDs, not only text.

## Diversity
Rotate among concepts, domains, activities, and parent perspectives. Repeated high-level advice must not crowd out less obvious but useful material.

## Developmental theme
Weekly and monthly outputs should synthesize related entries into a coherent developmental theme rather than randomize isolated tips.

## Safety
Warning signs are non-diagnostic. Health and safety guidance should be conservative and source-linked.

## Individual variation
Do not infer that a child is behind, ahead, gifted, deficient, or disordered solely from an Almanac entry.
