# 6–9 Research Source Registry

Research pass date: 2026-09-09

## Core developmental / educational sources

- `CDC-2026-MILESTONES`: CDC Learn the Signs. Act Early. milestone methodology. Used cautiously; CDC notes milestones are communication tools, not developmental standards. https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/key-points.html
- `NAEYC-DAP`: NAEYC Developmentally Appropriate Practice. Used for commonality/individuality/context, whole-child development, play/guided play, and intentional teaching. https://www.naeyc.org/node/3807
- `NAEYC-PRIMARY-PLAY`: NAEYC material on play and guided play in kindergarten through grade 3. https://www.naeyc.org/resources/pubs/yc/fall2026/growing-with-play
- `CASEL-FRAMEWORKS`: CASEL SEL competencies and grade-band framework resources. https://casel.org/state-resource-center/frameworks-competencies-standards-and-guidelines/
- `IES-MATH-ELEM`: U.S. Institute of Education Sciences practice guide for elementary mathematics interventions. Used for systematic instruction, mathematical language, and representations. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/26/Published
- `AASM-SLEEP`: American Academy of Sleep Medicine consensus recommendation: ages 6–12 generally need 9–12 hours of sleep per 24 hours. https://aasm.org/advocacy/position-statements/child-sleep-duration-health-advisory/
- `WHO-PA-5-17`: WHO physical-activity recommendation for ages 5–17: average at least 60 minutes/day of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity across the week. https://www.who.int/initiatives/behealthy/physical-activity
- `AAP-GRADESCHOOL-FITNESS`: AAP HealthyChildren school-age fitness guidance; ages 6–9 benefit from refining basic/transitional skills, fitness, and technique. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/gradeschool/fitness/Pages/default.aspx
- `AAP-NUTRITION-GRADESCHOOL`: AAP HealthyChildren nutrition guidance for school-age children. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/gradeschool/nutrition/Pages/default.aspx
- `AAP-MEDIA-SCHOOLAGE`: AAP HealthyChildren school-age media guidance and family media planning. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/Media/Pages/kids-and-screen-time-5-cs-questions-for-school-age-children.aspx
- `AAP-MEDIA-PLAN-2026`: AAP family media plan guidance, updated 2026. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/Media/Pages/How-to-Make-a-Family-Media-Use-Plan.aspx

## Source-use rule
A source may support an entry's general principle without supporting every concrete recommendation derived from it. Health, safety, and warning-sign claims require direct source support before entering the final corpus. This prototype intentionally excludes `healthy_development` and `warning_signs` so those categories can receive their own higher-scrutiny research pass.
