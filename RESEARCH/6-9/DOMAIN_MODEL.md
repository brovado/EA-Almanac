# 6–9 Domain Model

Every corpus entry receives one primary domain. Secondary tags may capture cross-domain relevance.

| Domain | Covers |
|---|---|
| academic | literacy, numeracy, learning, school habits, inquiry |
| character | persistence, integrity, fairness, courage, judgment |
| social | friendship, cooperation, conflict, belonging, perspective-taking |
| emotional | feelings, coping, frustration, self-concept, regulation |
| physical | movement, coordination, sleep, nutrition, safety, body confidence |
| responsibility | chores, commitments, organization, possessions, time |
| creativity | making, art, imagination, experimentation, expression |
| practical_life | self-care, food, money, household and community skills |

Domain balance is a retrieval preference, not a claim that development divides into isolated boxes. Content may carry secondary domain tags.
