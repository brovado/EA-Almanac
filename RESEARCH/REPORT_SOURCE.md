**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Scope:** 1–2 and 2–5; all 15 content categories.

## Result

The prototype corpus contains the requested category density: **1,125 entries for 1–2 and 1,125 entries for 2–5**. The supplied category counts sum to 1,125, not 1,175; the earlier 1,175 figure in the specification was an arithmetic error.

## Evidence model

CDC milestone checkpoints provide the observable developmental spine from 15 months through age 5. CDC describes milestones as things most children (75% or more) can do by a given age and cautions that milestone checklists are not substitutes for standardized screening.

WHO and UNICEF provide the broader nurturing-care frame: health, nutrition, safety/security, responsive caregiving, and opportunities for learning. WHO’s 2020 guideline specifically recommends responsive care and early-learning activities in the first three years.

NAEYC provides the educational framework: strengths-based, play-based practice; attention to common developmental patterns, individual variation, and family/community context; and sustained play as a central learning context.

Harvard’s Center on the Developing Child supplies the main framework for serve-and-return interaction and practicing executive-function/self-regulation skills.

AAP sources anchor practical parenting, positive discipline, media, feeding, oral health, sleep, and drowning prevention.

ALSC supplies the book-curation lane through library-professional recommendation lists, including current 2026 reading-list infrastructure.

## How the categories differ

Experiencing describes the developmental season. Needs describes the conditions that support it. Teach names capabilities. Encourage identifies behaviours and interests worth making room for. Avoid identifies counterproductive adult responses and preventable risks. Healthy Development provides observational examples. Warning Signs stays deliberately conservative and routes concern toward professional assessment. Weekly and Monthly Focus turn principles into manageable themes. Books and Activities supply concrete experiences. Conversations support responsive language and thinking. Life Skills decompose independence into teachable steps. Opportunities translate development into community and real-world experiences. Parent Reminders deliberately address the adult because the parent is part of the developmental environment.

## Important limitation

The current age bands are intentionally broad. They cross multiple CDC milestone checkpoints, so a later retrieval pass should attach finer age windows where that materially improves relevance.

Book and opportunity entries are recommendations rather than claims of universal superiority or guaranteed local availability.

Health, safety, and warning-sign content is intentionally conservative. Persistent regression, major functional difficulty, or consequential safety concerns should be routed to a qualified health or developmental professional.
